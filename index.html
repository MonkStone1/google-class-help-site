Google Class Help

A local desktop application for viewing and managing Google Classroom data.

What it does

Google Class Help connects to Google Classroom through the official Google APIs and lets you view your courses, coursework, submissions, and grades according to the permissions you grant during sign-in.

  • See your courses and coursework in one clear, organized view
  • Check due dates, submission status, and grades without digging through Classroom
  • Runs entirely on your own computer — no cloud account, no hosted service
  • Open any assignment in Google Classroom directly when you need more detail

How it works

You sign in with your Google account and authorize Google Class Help to read your Classroom data. The application talks to Google's servers only to fetch that data; everything you see is then stored and displayed locally on your own machine.

For details on exactly what data is accessed, why, and how it's handled, see the Privacy Policy.
