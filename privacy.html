Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Google Class Help is a local desktop application. This policy explains what Google Classroom data it accesses, why, and what happens to it.

What the application is

Google Class Help is installed and run on your own Windows computer. It is not a website, it does not run on a server operated by the developer, and it has no backend hosted in the cloud. All of the application's data processing — including talking to Google's servers and storing the results — happens on your machine.

What Google Classroom data the application accesses

When you sign in, Google asks you to approve a specific set of read-only permissions (scopes). The application only requests the following:

Scope What it allows
classroom.courses.readonly Read the list of your courses/classes and basic course details, and determine whether you are a student or a teacher in each one.
classroom.student-submissions.me.readonly Read coursework, due dates, your own submissions, and your grades for courses where you are a student — and, for courses where you are a teacher, the coursework and submission/grade information belonging to that course.
classroom.rosters.readonly Read the list of students enrolled in a course where you are the teacher, so that submissions and grades can be matched to the right student.
classroom.profile.emails (optional) Read a student's email address, requested only if and when the interface actually displays student emails.

Google Class Help only ever requests read-only access. It cannot create, edit, grade, or delete anything in Google Classroom on your behalf, and it never requests write-enabled scopes.

Why this data is needed

Each permission exists to power a specific part of the dashboard:

  • Course access lets the app show your subjects and, for teachers, tell teacher courses apart from student courses.
  • Coursework and submission access lets the app show assignments, due dates, statuses, and grades — the core purpose of the application.
  • Roster access lets a teacher account see which students are enrolled and match grades to the correct student.
  • Email access (only if used) lets student names be identified unambiguously where the interface shows email addresses.

What the application does with courses, assignments, grades, and submissions

This data is used strictly to display information back to you in a more convenient dashboard: lists of courses, upcoming and overdue assignments, submission status, and grades. For teacher accounts, the same data is used to show course-wide grade and submission overviews.

The application does not modify anything in Google Classroom. It does not post, submit, grade, or delete coursework, submissions, or grades. It does not share this data with other Google Classroom users, and no other user of Google Classroom can see data through this application.

Where data is stored

Everything the application retrieves — cached course, coursework, and grade data — is stored locally in a database on your own computer, inside your user-specific application data folder (for example, under %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoogleClassHelp\). It is never uploaded to any server operated by the developer, because no such server exists.

Your Google OAuth token, which the application uses to stay signed in, is also stored locally on your computer, in that same per-user application data folder (for example, %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoogleClassHelp\token.json). It is not embedded in the application, not shared between users on a shared computer, and not transmitted anywhere except directly to Google's own authentication servers, which is required for the sign-in to work.

Whether data is shared with third parties

No. Google Class Help does not send your Classroom data, your OAuth token, or any other personal information to the developer, to analytics providers, or to any other third party. The only party the application communicates with is Google's own API, and only to retrieve the data you've authorized it to read.

Whether data is used for advertising

No. Google Class Help contains no advertising, no ad SDKs, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Your data is never used, sold, or shared for advertising or any purpose unrelated to displaying your own Classroom information back to you.

How to delete your local data

Because everything is stored on your own computer, you are always in full control of it. You can:

  • Use the "Clear local cached data" option in the application's Settings page to remove the cached course, coursework, and grade data.
  • Use "Sign out" in Settings to remove your stored OAuth token from this computer, which also revokes the application's local access until you sign in again.
  • Manually delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoogleClassHelp\ folder to remove all locally stored data and the token at once.
  • Visit Google Account → Security → Third-party access to revoke Google Class Help's access to your Google account entirely.
